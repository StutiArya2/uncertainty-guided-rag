Caching stores the result of an expensive computation so it need not be repeated. Embedding caches are keyed by both the model identifier and a hash of the input, so a change to either produces a miss rather than a stale hit. Serving a stale cache entry after the underlying data has changed is a common and hard-to-diagnose bug. Prompt caching at the model provider level reduces cost for repeated prefixes. Cache invalidation strategy matters more than cache hit rate.
