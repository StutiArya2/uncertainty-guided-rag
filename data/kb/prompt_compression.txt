Prompt compression reduces the token cost of a prompt while preserving the information the model needs. Extractive compression selects a subset of the original sentences and discards the rest, so every surviving token is verbatim source text. Abstractive compression rewrites the content into a shorter paraphrase, achieving higher compression ratios at the cost of introducing text the source never contained. Extractive methods are straightforward to reverse because the discarded material can be reinstated exactly. Abstractive methods cannot be inverted from their output alone.
