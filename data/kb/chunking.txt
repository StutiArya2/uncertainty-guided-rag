Chunking splits documents into retrievable units. Chunks that are too small lack the context needed to interpret them, while chunks that are too large dilute the embedding and waste prompt tokens on irrelevant material. Sentence-level chunking is a common default for evidence-oriented tasks. Preserving each chunk's character offsets into its source document allows the exact original text to be recovered at any time. Without offsets, a chunk is merely a copy and its relationship to the source is lost.
