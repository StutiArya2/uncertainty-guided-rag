Hallucination refers to fluent model output that is not supported by any source. Intrinsic hallucinations contradict the provided context, while extrinsic hallucinations introduce claims the context simply does not address. Grounding generation in retrieved evidence reduces but does not eliminate the problem. Insufficient evidence is a common trigger: when context does not answer the question, models often fill the gap rather than declining. Verifying each claim against evidence before emitting an answer is a direct mitigation.
