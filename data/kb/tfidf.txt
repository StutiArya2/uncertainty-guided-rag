TF-IDF stands for term frequency inverse document frequency. It weights each term by how often it appears in a document, scaled down by how common that term is across the whole collection. Terms that appear in nearly every document, such as "the" or "and", receive weights close to zero. The scheme was introduced by Karen Sparck Jones in 1972 as a statistical interpretation of term specificity. TF-IDF remains a strong lexical baseline for retrieval despite being decades old.
