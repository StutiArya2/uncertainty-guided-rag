Claim decomposition breaks a complex statement into atomic factual units that can be verified independently. A sentence asserting two separate facts should become two claims, since evidence may support one and not the other. Decomposition can be applied to a user's question to produce sub-questions, or to a draft answer to produce checkable assertions. Verifying a draft answer checks exactly what will be shown to the user, but risks inheriting hallucinated claims. Decomposing the query instead avoids that circularity but may not cover everything the final answer asserts.
