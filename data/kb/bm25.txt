BM25 is a probabilistic ranking function used by search engines to estimate the relevance of documents to a query. It extends TF-IDF with two corrections: term frequency saturation, so repeating a word many times yields diminishing returns, and document length normalisation, so long documents are not unfairly favoured. The function has two tunable parameters, k1 and b, which control saturation and length normalisation respectively. Typical default values are k1 around 1.2 and b around 0.75. BM25 frequently matches or beats dense retrievers on out-of-domain benchmarks.
