An embedding is a dense vector representation of text in which semantic similarity corresponds to geometric proximity. Sentence embeddings are commonly produced by mean-pooling the token representations from a transformer encoder. Cosine similarity is the usual comparison metric because it ignores vector magnitude and compares only direction. The all-MiniLM-L6-v2 model produces 384-dimensional embeddings and is widely used for its speed. Embedding quality dominates retrieval quality in dense systems.
